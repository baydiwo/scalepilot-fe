# Crown ROAS: Marketing Pages Reference

This document serves as a structural reference and content guide for the Crown ROAS marketing website. It outlines the core messaging, objectives, and key points for each page under the **Features** and **Solutions** menus.

---

## 🚀 Menu: Features

These pages focus on the specific tools and capabilities of the Crown ROAS platform. They should be highly product-focused, showcasing the UI/UX and explaining *how* the technology works.

### 1. Verdict Engine
*   **Core Message:** Automated, data-driven decisions at your fingertips.
*   **What it is:** The proprietary algorithm that analyzes ad performance across multiple levels and tracks marginal scale.
*   **Key Highlights:**
    *   Level-Based Decision making.
    *   Continuous Marginal Scale Tracking.
    *   Removes emotional bias from ad management.
*   **Target Audience:** Media buyers and agency owners who are tired of manual data analysis.
*   **Primary CTA:** "See the Engine in Action" (Link to demo or sign-up).

### 2. Scale Tracker
*   **Core Message:** Grow your winning ads with confidence and control.
*   **What it is:** A visual and analytical tool to monitor campaigns specifically as their budgets are increased.
*   **Key Highlights:**
    *   Real-time ROAS monitoring during scaling phases.
    *   Alerts for ad fatigue or performance dips.
    *   Historical scaling timeline visualization.
*   **Target Audience:** E-commerce brands and agencies ready to scale their ad spend profitably.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Start Scaling Today"

### 3. Action Hub
*   **Core Message:** Your central command for all ad operations.
*   **What it is:** The unified dashboard where users execute, schedule, and review all ad changes.
*   **Key Highlights:**
    *   Bulk actions across multiple campaigns and ad sets.
    *   One-click optimizations based on Verdict Engine recommendations.
    *   Comprehensive audit log of all actions taken.
*   **Target Audience:** Ad managers looking to save hours of manual clicking in native Ads Managers.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Streamline Your Workflow"

### 4. Budget Revert
*   **Core Message:** Scale without fear. Undo mistakes instantly.
*   **What it is:** A safety net feature that allows users to instantly rollback budget increases if performance drops unexpectedly.
*   **Key Highlights:**
    *   One-click rollback to the previous stable budget.
    *   Automated safeguards against sudden ROAS drops.
    *   Peace of mind during aggressive scaling.
*   **Target Audience:** Risk-averse marketers, brand founders, and business owners.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Scale Safely Now"

## 💡 Menu: Solutions

These pages are outcome-driven. They should speak directly to the pain points of the user and explain *why* they need Crown ROAS, using the features as supporting evidence.

### 1. Make Decisions Faster
*   **The Problem:** Drowning in data, spreadsheets, and conflicting metrics delays critical ad decisions.
*   **The Solution:** Crown ROAS cuts through the noise, providing clear, actionable verdicts in seconds.
*   **Supporting Features:** Verdict Engine, Action Hub.
*   **Key Benefit:** Reclaim hours of your day and act on trends before your competitors do.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Speed Up Your Workflow"

### 2. Scale Winning Campaigns
*   **The Problem:** Increasing budgets often breaks performance, leading to lost momentum and revenue.
*   **The Solution:** A systematic, tracked approach to scaling that maximizes ROAS while spending more.
*   **Supporting Features:** Scale Tracker, Verdict Engine.
*   **Key Benefit:** Break through revenue plateaus predictably and profitably.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Unlock Your Scaling Potential"

### 3. Fix Underperforming Ads
*   **The Problem:** Bleeding money on ads that used to work, but you aren't sure why they stopped or how to fix them.
*   **The Solution:** Automated identification of ad fatigue and immediate actionable steps to pivot or pause.
*   **Supporting Features:** Verdict Engine, Action Hub.
*   **Key Benefit:** Turn losers off quickly and revive fatiguing creatives before they drain your budget.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Stop the Bleeding"

### 4. Reduce Wasted Ad Spend
*   **The Problem:** Leaving bad ads running too long and overspending on inefficient campaigns.
*   **The Solution:** Tight budget controls, instant rollbacks, and proactive pausing of inefficient spend.
*   **Supporting Features:** Budget Revert, Verdict Engine.
*   **Key Benefit:** Protect your profit margins and ensure every dollar is working hard for your business.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Protect Your Profit Margins"
